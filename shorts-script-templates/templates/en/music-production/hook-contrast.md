# Template: Hook + Before/After Contrast + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 25–40 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover / talking head  
**Retention:** High — contrast structure creates instant identification

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — "before" state the viewer recognizes in themselves]
[THE SHIFT — the one thing that changed]
[THE AFTER — concrete, believable result]
[LESSON — one sentence, no moralizing]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
Before, [behavior or belief the viewer recognizes]. Now, [opposite, concrete result].

**[THE SHIFT]**  
The only thing that changed was [one thing — idea, habit, approach]. Not [what people assume it was].

**[THE AFTER]**  
Since then, [tangible, specific result — avoid vague claims like "I feel better"].

**[LESSON]**  
You don't need [effort or resource people think is essential]. You just need [what actually matters].

**[CTA]**  
[Direct question that invites them to share their own experience].

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
Before, I spent hours on a beat and hated it by the end. Now I finish beats I'm proud of in under two hours.

**[THE SHIFT]**  
The only thing that changed was setting a timer. 90 minutes, then export whatever I have. No second-guessing, no endless tweaking.

**[THE AFTER]**  
Since then, I've released more music in 3 months than in the previous 2 years combined.

**[LESSON]**  
You don't need more plugins or a better setup. You need a deadline.

**[CTA]**  
How long do you usually spend on a beat before you call it done?

---

## Usage notes

- The "before" hook must sound like something the viewer would think about themselves, not an outsider's story
- The shift must be one thing only — multiple changes dilute the message
- Avoid generic advice ("be consistent", "trust the process") — specificity is what makes this format work
