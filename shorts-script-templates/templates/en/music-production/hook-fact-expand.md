# Template: Hook + Production Fact + Application + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 30–45 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover / screen share / talking head  
**Retention:** High in niche — producers and musicians are loyal when the tip is genuinely useful

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — statement that challenges something producers do by default]
[THE PROBLEM — the common habit and what it causes]
[THE FIX — the better approach, simply explained]
[PRACTICAL APPLICATION — what the viewer does with this]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
If you [common production habit], you're [negative consequence you didn't know about].

**[THE PROBLEM]**  
Most producers [default behavior]. The result is [technical or creative problem that creates].

**[THE FIX]**  
What works better is [alternative technique or approach — explained in 1–2 simple sentences].

**[APPLICATION]**  
Next time you [specific situation], try [specific action]. You'll hear the difference in the first [X] seconds.

**[CTA]**  
[Technical or creative question that invites producers to share their own experience].

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
If you mix with headphones on the whole time, you're mixing with lies.

**[THE PROBLEM]**  
Headphones create an artificial stereo image — bass sounds bigger, the center sounds wider. What you hear is not what the listener hears on speakers or in a car.

**[THE FIX]**  
Mix on monitors for frequency decisions and use headphones only to check fine details like sibilance or long reverb tails.

**[APPLICATION]**  
Next time you start mixing, plug in your monitors even if they're small. If you don't have any, export and listen on your phone speaker. That never lies.

**[CTA]**  
What do you mix on — headphones, monitors, or both?

---

## Usage notes

- The hook works best when it targets a very widespread habit (everyone does it this way)
- Avoid overly technical terms unless the channel is strictly for advanced producers
- The practical application tip is what makes people save the video
