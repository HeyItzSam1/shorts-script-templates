# Template: Hook + Surprising Fact + Expansion + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 25–40 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover / talking head  
**Retention:** Medium-high — works best when the hook fact genuinely breaks expectations

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — fact that breaks a common belief]
[CONTEXT — why people thought otherwise]
[EXPANSION — the real mechanism, simply explained]
[CLOSER — implication the viewer can apply or that affects them]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
[Counterintuitive claim about TOPIC]. And it makes complete sense.

**[CONTEXT]**  
For years, most people believed [common assumption]. But that turns out to be [wrong/incomplete/misleading].

**[EXPANSION]**  
The reason is [mechanism in 1–2 simple sentences, no jargon]. Which is why [observable consequence].

**[CLOSER]**  
What that means for you: [practical implication the viewer can act on or that directly affects them].

**[CTA]**  
[Question that invites a comment or prompts them to follow].

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
Drinking cold water after eating fatty food does not solidify fat in your arteries. That's a myth.

**[CONTEXT]**  
For decades this circulated in chain emails and social media posts. But there's no physiological basis for it.

**[EXPANSION]**  
Your body stays at 98.6°F regardless of what you drink. Cold water equalizes with your body temperature within seconds. What actually affects your arteries is the type of fat you eat and how often — not the temperature of your drink.

**[CLOSER]**  
What that means for you: keep drinking cold water. What you can't ignore is the quality of fat you're consuming.

**[CTA]**  
Follow if you want more data that destroys myths everyone believes.

---

## Usage notes

- The hook works best when it disproves something the viewer already believes is true
- Avoid technical terms — the expansion should be understood without rewinding
- The closer is the part people quote when they share the video
- No emojis if using AI voiceover (ElevenLabs, etc.)
