# Template: Hook + Mini Story + Twist + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 35–55 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover  
**Retention:** Very high — story structure with a twist activates the "I need to know how this ends" instinct

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — reveal the twist upfront, before the story]
[SETUP — minimum context to understand]
[TENSION — the critical moment]
[TWIST — the unexpected reveal or ending]
[CONNECTION TO TODAY — why it still matters]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
In [year/era], [person or group] [action that sounds incredible or absurd]. And it worked.

**[SETUP]**  
[Name/group] was [one-sentence description]. At the time, [context that explains why the situation was extreme].

**[TENSION]**  
When [the critical moment arrived], [what was at stake]. The decision: [action they took].

**[TWIST]**  
What nobody expected: [surprising outcome — 1–2 sentences].

**[CONNECTION]**  
Today [real consequence still alive: a law, an invention, a tradition, a place].

**[CTA]**  
Did you know this story? Drop your reaction below.

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
In 1962, NASA almost cancelled the Apollo program over a math error. One woman fixed it by hand.

**[SETUP]**  
Katherine Johnson was a mathematician at NASA back when computers were people, not machines. She calculated orbital trajectories with pencil and paper.

**[TENSION]**  
When Friendship 7 was ready to launch, the new IBM computers gave their coordinates. Astronaut John Glenn refused to board. He said: have Katherine check it first.

**[TWIST]**  
She found an error. The computers were wrong. Glenn launched. The mission was perfect.

**[CONNECTION]**  
Today her name is on a NASA building, and the methods she used are taught in universities worldwide.

**[CTA]**  
Did you know her story? Let me know below.

---

## Usage notes

- Reveal the twist in the hook — it drives more retention than saving it for the end
- Keep setup minimal: only what the viewer needs to understand the tension
- Avoid secondary dates and names that distract — max 2 proper names per script
- The connection to the present is what separates this from a random historical fact
