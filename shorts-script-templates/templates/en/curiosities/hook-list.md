# Template: Hook + Numbered List + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 30–50 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover / talking head  
**Retention:** High — the list creates curiosity loops that keep viewers watching

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — first 3 seconds]
[POINT 1]
[POINT 2]
[POINT 3]
[CLOSER / strongest fact]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
[Number] things about [TOPIC] that [X]% of people don't know.

**[POINT 1]**  
Number one: [surprising but short fact — max 2 sentences].

**[POINT 2]**  
Number two: [fact that escalates in intensity — max 2 sentences].

**[POINT 3]**  
Number three: [fact that reinforces the previous one — max 2 sentences].

**[CLOSER]**  
But the most insane one: [save your strongest fact for last].

**[CTA]**  
Which one surprised you most? Drop it in the comments.

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
3 things about the human brain that science still can't fully explain.

**[POINT 1]**  
Number one: your brain generates enough electricity to power a small light bulb while you sleep.

**[POINT 2]**  
Number two: forgetting is an active process. Your brain intentionally deletes information to work more efficiently.

**[POINT 3]**  
Number three: 95% of your decisions are made by your subconscious before you consciously "decide" anything.

**[CLOSER]**  
But the most disturbing one: you can't tickle yourself because your brain predicts the motion and cancels the sensation before it happens.

**[CTA]**  
Which one hit hardest? Let me know below.

---

## Usage notes

- The hook must work with zero context — the viewer knows nothing yet
- Each point: maximum 2 sentences. If you need more, the fact is too complex for Shorts
- Save your strongest fact for the end — that's what keeps people watching through the CTA
- No emojis in the script if using AI voiceover (ElevenLabs, etc.)
