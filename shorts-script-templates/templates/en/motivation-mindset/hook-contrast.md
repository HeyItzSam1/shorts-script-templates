# Template: Hook + Before/After Contrast + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 25–40 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover / talking head  
**Retention:** High — contrast activates immediate identification

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — "before" state the viewer recognizes in themselves]
[THE SHIFT — the one thing that changed]
[THE AFTER — concrete, believable result]
[LESSON — one sentence, no moralizing]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
Before, [behavior or belief the viewer recognizes]. Now, [opposite, concrete result].

**[THE SHIFT]**  
The only thing that changed was [one thing — idea, habit, perspective]. Not [what people usually assume].

**[THE AFTER]**  
Since then, [tangible, specific result — avoid vague claims like "I feel better"].

**[LESSON]**  
You don't need [effort or resource people think is essential]. You just need [what actually matters].

**[CTA]**  
[Direct question that invites them to share their own experience].

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
Before, I spent hours planning without doing anything. Now I get more done in 2 hours than I used to in a full day.

**[THE SHIFT]**  
The only thing that changed was stopping weekly planning. I just decide the night before what I'm doing tomorrow. One thing.

**[THE AFTER]**  
Since then, I haven't had a single blank day. The block disappears when the task is small enough that starting doesn't feel scary.

**[LESSON]**  
You don't need more discipline. You need smaller tasks.

**[CTA]**  
What's the first thing you're doing tomorrow? Write it below — now it's a commitment.

---

## Usage notes

- The "before" hook must sound like something the viewer would think about themselves
- The shift must be one thing only — multiple changes dilute the message
- Avoid generic motivational phrases ("believe in yourself", "stay consistent") — specificity is what makes this format work
- A CTA that asks for a concrete action (writing something) generates more comments than asking for opinions
