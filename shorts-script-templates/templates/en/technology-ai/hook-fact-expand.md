# Template: Hook + Tech Fact + Expansion + CTA

**Estimated duration:** 25–40 seconds  
**Format:** Voiceover / talking head  
**Retention:** High — works best when the hook challenges a widespread tech assumption

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — counterintuitive tech claim]
[WHAT PEOPLE THINK — the common assumption]
[WHAT'S ACTUALLY TRUE — simply explained]
[IMPLICATION — how this affects the viewer right now]
[CTA]
```

---

## Script

**[HOOK]**  
[Tech tool or platform] doesn't actually [what people think it does]. It [what it really does].

**[WHAT PEOPLE THINK]**  
Most users assume [common assumption]. So they [resulting behavior that's actually suboptimal].

**[WHAT'S ACTUALLY TRUE]**  
In reality, [the real mechanism in 1–2 sentences without technical jargon].

**[IMPLICATION]**  
Which means [concrete change in how the viewer should use this tool or think about it].

**[CTA]**  
[Question that invites them to share how they use the tool or what they assumed before].

---

## Filled example

**[HOOK]**  
Google doesn't rank your page for having more keywords. It ranks it for answering questions better than everyone else.

**[WHAT PEOPLE THINK]**  
Most people think SEO is about repeating the right words enough times. So they stuff their content with keywords and wonder why nothing ranks.

**[WHAT'S ACTUALLY TRUE]**  
Google's algorithm measures how long people stay on your page, whether they click back to search, and whether other sites reference you. Keywords are the entry point, not the ranking factor.

**[IMPLICATION]**  
Which means the best thing you can do for SEO is write something people actually finish reading and share.

**[CTA]**  
What did you think SEO was before you learned this? Drop it below.

---

## Usage notes

- The hook works best as a direct correction: "X doesn't do Y, it does Z"
- Keep the explanation free of technical SEO/AI/dev jargon — if the viewer needs to Google a term, you've lost them
- The implication should be actionable in the next 24 hours, not a long-term strategy
- Pair with a screen recording or graphic if posting as a video (not just audio)
